Chance Lab

Set sample parameters and watch as the results trend toward the mathematical expectation.

Made for a stats period. No account. Nothing to install. Works on a projector or smartboard.

  • Settle to fifty — If a coin is fair, why can the first 20 flips look nothing like 50/50?
  • A loaded coin — How many flips before you can see that the coin is cheating?
  • Why seven wins — Two dice. Why does 7 keep beating 2 and 12?
  • The 1/3 surprise — You eliminate one of the combinations. How does that change the probabilities for the remaining ones?
  • Given a six — You know you have one six. How does that change the odds?

Solid bars show the sample results. Fine horizontal lines indicate the mathematical expectation.
